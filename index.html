Country-Wise Passport and Visa Photo Size Guide

India Passport Photo Size

Indian passport photo size is 3.5 cm x 4.5 cm (35 mm x 45 mm). This size is required for Indian passport application, Indian visa application, OCI card, PIO card, Aadhaar card, PAN card, and most Indian government ID documents. White background is mandatory for Indian passport photos.

USA Passport Photo Size

US passport photo size is 2 inches x 2 inches (5.08 cm x 5.08 cm or 51 mm x 51 mm). This is required for US passport application, US visa application (B1/B2, H1B, L1, F1, J1, etc.), Green Card application, and US immigration documents. White background required.

UK Passport Photo Size

United Kingdom passport photo size is 3.5 cm x 4.5 cm (35 mm x 45 mm). Required for UK passport renewal, UK visa application, and British identity documents. Light grey or cream background preferred.

Canada Passport and Visa Photo Size

Canadian passport photo size is 3.5 cm x 4.5 cm (35 mm x 45 mm). Same size used for Canada visa application, Canada PR card, Canadian citizenship application, and Study Permit. White or light coloured background required.

Schengen Visa Photo Size

Schengen visa photo size is 3.5 cm x 4.5 cm (35 mm x 45 mm). This standard applies to all Schengen area countries including Germany, France, Italy, Spain, Netherlands, Belgium, Austria, Switzerland, Greece, Portugal, Sweden, Norway, Denmark, Finland, Czech Republic, Poland, Hungary, and others. Light background required.

Australia Passport Photo Size

Australian passport photo size is 3.5 cm x 4.5 cm (35 mm x 45 mm). Used for Australian passport application, Australian visa, and immigration documents. White or light background.

UAE Visa Photo Size

UAE visa photo size is 4.0 cm x 6.0 cm (40 mm x 60 mm). Required for UAE tourist visa, employment visa, residence visa, and Dubai visa application. White background mandatory.

Pakistan Passport Photo Size

Pakistan passport photo size is 3.5 cm x 4.5 cm (35 mm x 45 mm). Used for Pakistani passport, NICOP, and visa applications. White or blue background.

Nepal Passport Photo Size

Nepal passport photo size is 3.5 cm x 4.5 cm (35 mm x 45 mm). Required for Nepali passport and visa applications.
